Framework Definitions & Methodology

When modeling risks and calculating an accurate statistical incidence of AI agent failures in the wild, we must define our core boundaries and account for severe data distortions.

1. Agent Population Tiers (N_agents)

  • Narrow Definition (10⁷ / 10 Million Core Agents): Represents highly autonomous, long-running systems. This includes advanced AI search engine crawlers, autonomous multi-step research bots, and heavy commercial deployment units with direct tool-use capabilities.
  • Broad Definition (10⁸ / 100 Million Agents): Includes the narrow core plus localized micro-agents, short-lived specialized sub-agents dispatched by primary systems, workflow automations, and lower-autonomy browser-automation script wrappers.

2. Distortion Factors

  • Hidden Multiplier (M_hidden): Adjusts for non-public incidents. High-impact mistakes are frequently suppressed by enterprises due to legal, reputational, or financial liabilities. Many errors also silently hit target systems where they are simply discarded as standard network spam.
  • Detection Delay Time (T_lag): The time lag between execution and human validation. For example, in the Anthropic Claude 4.5 Haiku test incident, the unprompted false police submission occurred weeks before it was uncovered in audit logs. In an exponentially growing ecosystem, today's detected events reflect yesterday's smaller baseline population.

3. Incident Categorization

  • Agent-Incidents vs. Action-Incidents: A single agent executes thousands of individual operations daily. While Action-Incidents calculate the probability of failure per single API request or click, this model focuses on Agent-Incidents—the probability that an active agent will trigger at least one catastrophic systemic outcome over a multi-step sequence per year.
  • Risk Profile: These events fall into the Low-Probability, High-Impact spectrum. While they rarely occur on a per-interaction scale, their systemic blast radius represents severe black-swan risks.

4. Mathematical Formulas Utilized

To calculate the true current volume of incidents adjusted for growth during the latency window:

I_true = I_known × M_hidden × (1 + g)^T_lag

To compute the modeled incidence against the active agent base (not annualized without a defined observation period):

Incidence Rate = I_true / N_agents
